# Biljard-page

# Billiards Community

A social platform for billiards players to connect, discuss, compete, and improve their game.

# Discover & Discuss

Join conversations about pool, snooker, and other billiards games. Create posts, ask questions, share tips, and discuss everything from techniques and equipment to tournaments and professional players.

# Find Players

Connect with other billiards players in your area or around the world. Find opponents, training partners, and new people to play with.

# Share Your Game

Post match results, achievements, photos, videos, and memorable shots. Follow other players and stay up to date with the billiards community.

# Tournaments & Matches

Discover upcoming tournaments and events, organize your own matches, and keep track of results and rankings.

# Improve Your Skills

Exchange training tips, practice routines, strategies, and advice with other players. Learn from the community and take your game to the next level.

# Community Profiles

Create your own player profile, showcase your experience and achievements, follow other players, and build your reputation within the community.

One place for billiards players to connect, compete, share, and improve.

# Installation

Run "sqlite3 database.db < sql/schema.sql" to initialize the database.
Run "sqlite3 database.db < sql/populate.sql" to populate the database.
Create a new virtual environment with "python3 -m venv .venv"
Activate the venv with "source venv/bin/activate"
Run "pip install -r requirements.txt" to install project dependencies.
Call "flask run" to launch the server.
Go to "localhost:5000" in a web browser to access the site.
